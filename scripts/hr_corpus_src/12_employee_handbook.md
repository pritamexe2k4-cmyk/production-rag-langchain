## 1. About Acme Analytics
1.1 Acme Analytics Pvt Ltd was founded in 2014. It builds data and analytics products for retail and banking clients in India and Southeast Asia. In 2026 the company has about 850 employees.
1.2 The headquarters is in Hitech City, Hyderabad. There are two other offices: Bengaluru (Outer Ring Road) and Pune (Hinjewadi).
1.3 Our values are Integrity, Respect, Ownership and Customer Focus. They are explained in the Code of Conduct.

## 3. Bands and levels
Acme uses five bands. Many policies, such as leave, expenses, insurance and notice period, depend on the band.

| Band | Typical titles | Typical experience |
|---|---|---|
| Band A | Associate, Analyst, Software Engineer | 0 to 3 years |
| Band B | Senior Analyst, Senior Engineer | 3 to 7 years |
| Band C | Lead, Manager, Principal Engineer | 7 to 12 years |
| Band D | Senior Manager, Director | 12 to 18 years |
| Band E | Vice President and above | 18 years and above |

## 4. Working hours and attendance
4.1 Standard office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Core hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, when everyone should be available.
4.2 Most roles follow the hybrid model: 3 days in the office and 2 days remote, with Tuesday and Wednesday as anchor days. The detailed rules are in the Hybrid Work and Work From Home Policy.
4.3 Attendance is recorded through the access card and PeopleHub. Late arrival of more than 3 times in a month will be discussed by the manager.
4.4 Teams that support customers outside Indian business hours work in shifts. A shift allowance is paid as defined in the offer letter.

## 5. Probation and confirmation
5.1 New permanent employees are on probation for 6 months. The probation can be extended by up to 3 months if performance is not yet at the expected level.
5.2 Confirmation is given in writing after the manager's review. During probation the notice period is 30 days, and Privilege Leave is limited to 3 consecutive days at a time.

## 8. Grievance redressal
8.1 An employee who has a work-related grievance should first talk to the reporting manager. The manager must respond within 5 working days.
8.2 If the issue is not solved, the employee can take it to the HR business partner, who responds within 5 working days.
8.3 If it is still not solved, the employee can write to the HR head, who gives a decision within 10 working days.
8.4 Serious matters such as harassment, fraud and discrimination can go straight to the Ethics Helpline or the Internal Committee, without going through these levels.

## 9. The policy library
The policies that apply to you are on PeopleHub under "Policies". They are: Leave Policy, Hybrid Work and Work From Home Policy, Code of Conduct, POSH Policy, Expense and Travel Reimbursement Policy, Performance Review Policy, Notice Period and Exit Policy, Compensation and Payroll Policy, IT Security and Acceptable Use Policy, the Employee Benefits and Insurance Guide, the Onboarding Guide and the yearly Holiday List. If two documents differ, the policy with the later effective date prevails, unless a document says otherwise.

## 10. Changes to policies
Acme may change its policies from time to time. HR will inform employees by email at least 15 days before a change that reduces a benefit takes effect.
