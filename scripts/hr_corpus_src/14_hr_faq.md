## 1. Leave and holidays
### 1.1 How many leaves do I get in a year?
Everyone gets 8 days of Casual Leave and 8 days of Sick Leave per year. Privilege Leave depends on your band: 18 days in Band A, 20 in Band B, 22 in Band C, 24 in Band D and 26 in Band E. See the Leave Policy.

### 1.2 Can I carry forward my leave?
Only Privilege Leave can be carried forward, up to 30 days in Bands A and B, 36 days in Band C, and 45 days in Bands D and E. Casual Leave and Sick Leave lapse on 31 December.

### 1.3 How do I take half a day off?
Apply for a half-day leave in PeopleHub. A half day counts as 0.5 day.

### 1.4 How many holidays do we have in 2027?
There are 11 company holidays in each office, and 2 optional holidays that you can choose from the list of 6. See the Holiday List 2027.

## 2. Working from home
### 2.1 How many days do I need to be in the office?
Three days a week. Tuesday and Wednesday are anchor days. During your first 60 days, you need to be in the office 4 days a week.

### 2.2 Can I work from my hometown?
Yes, for up to 20 working days in a calendar year within India, with your manager's approval. Working from outside India needs approval from Legal and Finance.

## 3. Money matters
### 3.1 When is the salary credited?
On the last working day of the month. The payroll cut-off is the 20th.

### 3.2 When do I get my reimbursement?
If your claim is fully approved by the 20th, it is paid with that month's salary. Otherwise it is paid in the next month. Submit claims within 30 days of the expense.

### 3.3 When is the variable pay paid?
With the June salary. The increments take effect on 1 July.

### 3.4 What is my hotel limit when I travel for work?
It depends on your band and the city. For example, in a metro city it is Rs 4,500 per night for Band A and Rs 8,000 for Band C. The full table is in the Expense and Travel Reimbursement Policy.

## 4. Benefits
### 4.1 Is my family covered under health insurance?
Yes. You, your spouse and up to two children are covered by the company from day 1. You can add your parents by paying an extra premium.

### 4.2 Does Acme provide childcare or a creche?
No. There is currently no creche or childcare reimbursement.

### 4.3 What is the referral bonus?
Rs 30,000 for hires in Band A and B and Rs 50,000 for Band C and above, paid after the referred person completes 6 months.

## 5. Conduct and safety
### 5.1 Can I accept a gift from a client?
A gift of up to Rs 1,000 can be accepted. For Rs 1,001 to Rs 3,000, you must declare it within 7 days. Gifts above Rs 3,000 must be declined or returned. Cash and gift cards are never accepted.

### 5.2 Whom do I contact if I face harassment?
You can write to the Internal Committee at ic@acme-analytics.example. For other ethics concerns, contact the Ethics Helpline on 1800-000-1234.

### 5.3 Can I do freelance work on weekends?
Paid work for another employer or a business needs the written approval of the HR head. Without approval, it is moonlighting and is not allowed.

## 6. Leaving Acme
### 6.1 What is my notice period?
It depends on your band: 30 days if you are on probation, 60 days for Bands A and B, 90 days for Bands C and D, and 120 days for Band E.

### 6.2 Can I use my leave during the notice period?
Privilege Leave cannot be used to shorten the notice. Any leave other than approved sick leave extends the notice period by the same number of days.

### 6.3 When will I get my final settlement?
Within 45 days of your last working day.

## 7. IT
### 7.1 Can I use ChatGPT for my work?
Only for approved AI tools listed on the intranet. Never enter client data, source code or personal data into public AI tools.

### 7.2 I lost my laptop. What do I do?
Report it within 2 hours to security@acme-analytics.example and the IT helpdesk.
