# Hybrid Work and Work From Home Policy
Document type: policy
Category: wfh
Effective date: 2026-07-01
Version: 2.1
Owner: Human Resources and Workplace Operations, Acme Analytics Pvt Ltd
Note: Fictional sample document created for testing a RAG system. Acme Analytics Pvt Ltd is not a real company.

## 1. Purpose
1.1 Acme Analytics Pvt Ltd follows a hybrid model. Employees spend part of the week in the office to collaborate, and part of the week working remotely to focus and to save commute time.
1.2 This policy sets the rules for the hybrid schedule, working from another city, fully remote roles, remote-work allowances and the expectations from employees and managers.

## 2. The standard hybrid schedule
2.1 The default schedule is 3 days in the office and 2 days remote each week.
2.2 Tuesday and Wednesday are anchor days. All employees in hybrid roles must be in the office on both anchor days. The third office day is chosen by the team with the manager's approval.
2.3 Working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees must be available online during core hours, which are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST, on remote days as well as office days.
2.4 Remote days must be marked in PeopleHub by 10:00 AM on the day. Attendance on office days is recorded through the access card.
2.5 Remote days that are not used cannot be accumulated or carried to another week.

## 3. Eligibility
| Employee stage | Office days per week | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| First 60 days after joining | 4 days | Needed for onboarding, buddy support and equipment setup |
| Probation (after day 60) | 3 days | Same as the standard schedule |
| Confirmed employees in hybrid roles | 3 days | Tuesday and Wednesday are anchor days |
| Fully remote roles | 0 days (as approved) | Only for roles marked "remote-eligible"; needs VP approval |

## 4. Fully remote roles
4.1 A small number of roles are marked remote-eligible by the business. A fully remote arrangement must be approved in writing by the Vice President of the function and the Human Resources head.
4.2 Fully remote employees must travel to the nearest Acme office for quarterly team meetings. Travel for these meetings is reimbursed under the Expense and Travel Reimbursement Policy.
4.3 A fully remote arrangement can be withdrawn with 30 days notice if the role changes or performance concerns arise.

## 5. Working from another city (Work From Anywhere)
5.1 Employees in hybrid roles may work remotely from a city other than their office city for up to 20 working days in a calendar year, with the prior approval of their manager.
5.2 Work From Anywhere is allowed only within India. Working from outside India is not permitted without written approval from Legal and Finance, because of tax, labour-law and data-protection implications.
5.3 The employee must request at least 5 working days in advance and must be reachable during core hours.

## 6. Emergency work from home
When the office is not accessible because of severe weather, a transport strike or a government advisory, Human Resources will announce emergency work from home. In such cases the anchor-day rule is suspended. An employee who has a personal emergency (for example, a home repair or a child's sickness) may work from home for up to 2 days in a month with the manager's approval, in addition to the regular remote days.

## 7. Allowances and equipment
| Item | Entitlement | How to claim |
|---|---|---|
| Broadband allowance | Rs 1,000 per month for employees in hybrid and remote roles | Submit the broadband bill monthly in ExpenseDesk |
| Home office setup | One-time Rs 10,000, once every 3 years | Claim with invoices in ExpenseDesk, see the Expense and Travel Reimbursement Policy |
| Laptop and accessories | Company laptop issued by IT | Raise a request with the IT helpdesk |

Rs 1,000 per month is paid against the bill only up to the amount of the actual broadband bill. Employees who receive a mobile allowance cannot claim the same bill twice.

## 8. Security and data protection when working remotely
8.1 Only company-issued laptops may be used for work. Personal laptops must not be used to store or process company data.
8.2 The company VPN must be switched on whenever the employee is connected to any network outside an Acme office.
8.3 Public Wi-Fi in cafes, airports and hotels must not be used for work without the VPN. See the IT Security and Acceptable Use Policy for details.
8.4 Confidential calls must not be taken in public places. Screens must be locked when the employee steps away.

## 9. Manager responsibilities
9.1 Managers must keep team schedules fair and must not penalise employees for using their remote days.
9.2 Managers must make sure that at least half of the team is in the office on any anchor day.
9.3 Managers should not ask a team member to give up approved remote days at short notice, except for client commitments.

## 10. Non-compliance
10.1 If an employee does not meet the office-day requirement without approval, the manager will first have a conversation with the employee.
10.2 Repeated non-compliance (more than 3 missed office days in a quarter) will be recorded in the performance review and may lead to withdrawal of remote days.
10.3 Office days missed for approved leave, business travel or a public holiday do not count as non-compliance.
