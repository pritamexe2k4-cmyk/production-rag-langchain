## 1. Introduction
This guide describes the benefits that Acme Analytics Pvt Ltd offers to its permanent employees in India. The policy documents, insurance policy wording and the law prevail in case of a difference.

## 2. Group Medical Insurance
### 2.1 Who is covered
2.1.1 The company pays the premium for the employee, the spouse and up to 2 dependent children, from the first day of employment.
2.1.2 Parents or parents-in-law can be covered by paying the extra premium, which is deducted from the employee's salary in monthly instalments. The sum insured for parents is Rs 3,00,000 (3 lakh) as a separate cover.

### 2.2 Sum insured by band
The sum insured is a family floater, that is, one amount shared by the employee, spouse and children.

| Band | Sum insured (family floater) |
|---|---|
| Band A | Rs 4,00,000 |
| Band B | Rs 5,00,000 |
| Band C | Rs 7,50,000 |
| Band D | Rs 10,00,000 |
| Band E | Rs 15,00,000 |

### 2.3 What is covered
Hospitalisation of more than 24 hours, day-care procedures, pre-hospitalisation expenses for 30 days and post-hospitalisation expenses for 60 days, ambulance charges up to Rs 3,000, pre-existing diseases from day 1 and COVID-19 treatment. Maternity cover is Rs 75,000 for normal delivery and Rs 1,00,000 for a caesarean delivery, with no waiting period. Newborn babies are covered from day 1 if added within 30 days.

### 2.4 What is not covered
Cosmetic surgery, infertility treatment, dental treatment unless it is due to an accident, and expenses for non-medical items. Please read the policy document for the full list.

### 2.5 Enrolment and changes
New joiners must add their dependants within 15 days of joining. Other changes are possible only in the annual enrolment window in April, or within 30 days of a life event such as marriage or the birth of a child.

### 2.6 Making a claim
Cashless claims can be made at network hospitals using the health card. For planned hospitalisation, inform the insurance desk 3 days before; for emergencies, within 24 hours of admission. For reimbursement claims, submit the documents to the TPA, MediAssist Health TPA, within 30 days of discharge. Help is available at benefits@acme-analytics.example.

## 3. Life and accident cover
| Cover | Amount |
|---|---|
| Group Term Life Insurance | 4 times the annual fixed pay, up to a maximum of Rs 1 crore |
| Group Personal Accident (death or permanent total disability) | 3 times the annual fixed pay |

The premium for both covers is paid fully by the company. The cover ends on the last working day.

## 4. Wellness and mental health
4.1 An annual preventive health check-up is free for the employee at a partner diagnostic centre. For the spouse, the company pays up to Rs 5,000 towards the check-up.
4.2 The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) is a confidential helpline open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for personal, family and workplace concerns. Each employee and each family member can have 6 free counselling sessions per year.
4.3 A fitness reimbursement of up to Rs 6,000 per year is available for gym or yoga membership on receipts.

## 5. Learning and development
5.1 Every employee has a learning budget of Rs 25,000 per year for courses and certifications that relate to their role. The manager approves the request before enrolment, and the reimbursement is made on successful completion.
5.2 Employees also receive 2 paid learning days per year to prepare for exams and certifications.
5.3 If an employee leaves within 12 months of getting a certification reimbursed above Rs 10,000, the amount is recovered on a pro-rata basis.

## 6. Provident Fund, gratuity and NPS
6.1 The employee and the employer each contribute 12% of the Basic salary to the Provident Fund.
6.2 Gratuity is paid after 5 years of continuous service, as described in the Notice Period and Exit Policy.
6.3 Employees may choose the National Pension System (NPS). Acme contributes up to 10% of the Basic salary to NPS as part of the Cost to Company, which can give tax benefits under the applicable regime.

## 7. Meals and transport
7.1 Tea, coffee and snacks are free in all offices. Lunch in the cafeteria is subsidised.
7.2 Employees can opt for meal vouchers of up to Rs 2,200 per month within their salary structure.
7.3 A drop-home cab is provided to any employee who works in the office after 9:00 PM. Women employees travelling by company cab after 8:00 PM are accompanied by a security escort, as per the safety guidelines.

## 8. Employee referral programme
Employees get a referral bonus when a person they referred joins and completes 6 months. The bonus is Rs 30,000 for hires in Band A and Band B, and Rs 50,000 for hires in Band C and above. Referral of relatives who would report to the same manager is not allowed. The bonus is paid with the next payroll after the 6-month completion.

## 9. Other benefits
Long-service awards are given at 5, 10 and 15 years. A gift voucher of Rs 5,000 is given on the birth of a child and Rs 10,000 on a marriage. There is no creche facility in the offices at present; a childcare reimbursement is not provided.
