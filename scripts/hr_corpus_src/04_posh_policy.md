## 1. Purpose and legal basis
1.1 Acme Analytics Pvt Ltd is committed to a workplace that is free from sexual harassment. This policy is framed under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 and the rules made under it.
1.2 The policy applies to every employee, whether permanent, fixed-term, intern, trainee, consultant or contractor, and to visitors and vendors who interact with Acme employees. It applies at the office, at client sites, during business travel, at company events and in virtual spaces such as video calls, chat and email.

## 2. What is sexual harassment
2.1 Sexual harassment includes any unwelcome act or behaviour, direct or by implication, such as:
- physical contact and advances;
- a demand or request for sexual favours;
- sexually coloured remarks;
- showing pornography;
- any other unwelcome physical, verbal or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature.
2.2 It also includes implied or explicit promise of preferential treatment, threat of detrimental treatment, threat about present or future employment status, interference with work, or creating an intimidating or hostile work environment.
2.3 Intent is not the test. What matters is how the behaviour is received. If it is unwelcome and of a sexual nature, it may be harassment.

## 3. The Internal Committee (IC)

## 4. How to make a complaint
4.1 An aggrieved person can make a written complaint to the Internal Committee, with supporting documents and the names of witnesses if any. If the person cannot write, the Presiding Officer or any IC member will help in writing the complaint.
4.2 The complaint must be made within 3 months of the date of the incident, or the last incident in a series of incidents. The Internal Committee can extend this time by another 3 months if it is satisfied that circumstances prevented the person from filing the complaint in time.
4.3 If the aggrieved person is unable to complain because of physical or mental incapacity or death, a legal heir or any other person authorised by them may file the complaint.
4.4 An employee may also approach the Local Committee of the district if the complaint is against the senior-most person of the organisation.

## 5. Process and timelines
| Stage | Time limit |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement of complaint | Within 3 working days |
| Optional conciliation (settlement) before inquiry | At the request of the aggrieved person; no monetary settlement is permitted as the basis of conciliation |
| Copy of complaint sent to the respondent | Within 7 days of receipt |
| Respondent's reply | Within 10 working days of receiving the copy |
| Completion of the inquiry | Within 90 days |
| Inquiry report submitted to Acme | Within 10 days of completing the inquiry |
| Action by Acme on the report | Within 60 days of receiving the report |
| Appeal against the decision | Within 90 days of the recommendations |

5.1 The Internal Committee will hear both sides, give each party a chance to present their case and examine witnesses. Each party may be accompanied by a colleague for support but not by a lawyer.
5.2 Both parties are informed of the findings and given a copy of the report.

## 6. Interim relief
During the inquiry, on a written request by the aggrieved person, the Internal Committee can recommend that Acme:
- transfer the aggrieved person or the respondent to another team or location;
- grant the aggrieved person leave of up to 3 months, in addition to the leave she is entitled to;
- restrain the respondent from reporting on the work performance of the aggrieved person;
- provide any other relief the committee considers appropriate.

## 7. Action if harassment is proved
If the Internal Committee finds the allegation proved, Acme will act in line with the Code of Conduct and the Act. Possible actions are a written apology, a warning, withholding of an increment or promotion, community service, counselling, suspension, and termination of services. Acme may also deduct from the respondent's salary an amount to compensate the aggrieved person, as decided by the committee.

## 8. Confidentiality and protection against retaliation
8.1 The identity of the aggrieved person, the respondent, witnesses and the details of the inquiry are strictly confidential. Disclosing them is itself an offence under the Act and a violation of this policy, and can lead to a penalty.
8.2 Retaliation or victimisation of a person who files a complaint or a witness is not allowed. Any such act will be dealt with as gross misconduct.
8.3 If a complaint is found to be false and malicious, or if false evidence is given, action may be taken against that person. A complaint that cannot be proved is not, by itself, a false complaint.

## 9. Training and awareness
9.1 Every new employee must complete the POSH e-learning module within 30 days of joining.
9.2 All employees must complete a refresher once every year, by 31 March.
9.3 The Internal Committee members attend an annual training workshop on conducting inquiries.
9.4 Acme conducts an awareness session for managers every year and displays the names and contact details of the Internal Committee members in each office and on PeopleHub.

## 10. Annual report
The Internal Committee prepares an annual report with the number of complaints received, disposed of and pending for more than 90 days. This is shared with the Board and with the district authorities as required by law.
