# Performance Review Policy
Document type: policy
Category: performance
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Version: 2.4
Owner: Human Resources and Talent Management, Acme Analytics Pvt Ltd
Note: Fictional sample document created for testing a RAG system. Acme Analytics Pvt Ltd is not a real company.

## 1. Purpose
1.1 The performance review process helps employees understand how they are doing, recognises good work, and decides on increments, variable pay and promotions in a fair way.
1.2 This policy applies to all confirmed employees. New joiners in probation follow the probation review described in the Onboarding Guide.

## 2. The review cycle
2.1 The performance year runs from 1 April to 31 March.
2.2 Key dates in a year:
- Goal setting: by 30 April, using OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) agreed between the employee and the manager;
- Mid-year check-in: October, a conversation without a rating;
- Self-assessment: first two weeks of March;
- Manager assessment and calibration: March to April;
- Rating communication: by 30 April;
- Increments and promotions take effect on 1 July;
- Variable pay is paid in June.

## 3. Eligibility
3.1 Employees who joined on or before 30 September of the performance year are eligible for the annual rating and the increment.
3.2 Employees who joined after 30 September are reviewed in the next cycle and get a pro-rata review of their goals at the mid-year check-in. They are not eligible for the annual increment but may be considered for variable pay on a pro-rata basis.
3.3 An employee on notice period, or on Leave Without Pay for more than 90 days in the performance year, is not eligible for the rating-linked increment.

## 4. Rating scale
| Rating | Label | Meaning | Target share of employees |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Outstanding | Exceptional results; role model for others | Up to 10% |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations | Regularly goes beyond the goals | About 25% |
| 3 | Meets expectations | Delivers the goals reliably | About 50% |
| 2 | Partially meets | Some goals missed; needs improvement | About 12% |
| 1 | Does not meet | Most goals missed; serious concern | Up to 3% |

The target share is a guide used during calibration, not a quota for any single team.

## 5. Increment guidelines
| Rating | Increment range on fixed pay |
|---|---|
| 5 | 12% to 15% |
| 4 | 9% to 11% |
| 3 | 6% to 8% |
| 2 | 0% to 3% |
| 1 | No increment |

Promotions are separate from the rating. An employee needs a rating of 4 or higher in at least one of the last two years, and must be performing at the next level, to be considered for promotion.

## 6. Variable pay
6.1 Employees are eligible for a variable pay with a target percentage of their annual fixed pay, based on band.

| Band | Target variable pay (% of annual fixed pay) |
|---|---|
| Band A | 5% |
| Band B | 8% |
| Band C | 12% |
| Band D | 18% |
| Band E | 25% |

6.2 The payout is the target amount multiplied by a company performance factor, decided every year by the Board, and by an individual multiplier based on the rating: 1.5 for rating 5, 1.2 for rating 4, 1.0 for rating 3, 0.5 for rating 2 and 0 for rating 1.
6.3 Variable pay is paid with the June payroll. An employee who has resigned before the payout date is not eligible for variable pay.

## 7. Calibration and feedback
7.1 Managers propose ratings. A calibration committee of department heads and the HR business partner reviews them for consistency across teams.
7.2 For Band C and above, feedback from peers and direct reports (360-degree feedback) is collected and shared with the manager.
7.3 Managers must give the employee written feedback at the time of rating communication.

## 8. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
8.1 An employee who gets a rating of 1, or a rating of 2 in two consecutive years, is placed on a Performance Improvement Plan.
8.2 A PIP lasts 60 days. It has clear goals, weekly check-ins and the support needed to improve.
8.3 If the goals of the PIP are met, the employee returns to the normal cycle. If they are not met, the company may end the employment with notice, as described in the Notice Period and Exit Policy.
8.4 An employee on a PIP is not eligible for an increment, promotion or internal transfer.

## 9. Appeals
An employee who disagrees with the rating may write to the HR business partner within 7 working days of the rating communication. The HR head reviews the appeal with the calibration committee and gives a decision within 15 working days. The decision of the HR head is final.
