# Compensation and Payroll Policy
Document type: policy
Category: payroll
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Version: 2.2
Owner: Finance and Payroll, Acme Analytics Pvt Ltd
Note: Fictional sample document created for testing a RAG system. Acme Analytics Pvt Ltd is not a real company.

## 1. Pay structure
1.1 Compensation is expressed as Cost to Company (CTC) per year. It consists of fixed pay and, for eligible employees, variable pay.
1.2 Fixed pay is made up of: Basic salary, which is 40% of fixed pay; House Rent Allowance (HRA), which is 50% of Basic in the metro cities of Hyderabad, Bengaluru and Pune; employer contribution to the Provident Fund; and a special allowance for the remaining amount.
1.3 The target variable pay depends on the band and is explained in the Performance Review Policy.

## 2. Payroll calendar
2.1 Salary is credited on the last working day of each month.
2.2 The payroll cut-off is the 20th of every month. Leave, attendance changes and approved reimbursement claims received by the 20th are processed in the same month's payroll.
2.3 Payslips are available in PeopleHub within 3 working days after the salary is credited.
2.4 If the last working day falls on a bank holiday, the salary is credited on the previous working day.

## 3. Deductions
3.1 Statutory deductions include the employee's Provident Fund contribution of 12% of Basic, Professional Tax as per the state law (Rs 200 per month in Telangana, Karnataka and Maharashtra), and income tax deducted at source (TDS).
3.2 Other deductions may include recovery of salary advances, excess leave taken, and unreturned company assets.

## 4. Income tax
4.1 TDS is calculated under the new tax regime by default. An employee can choose the old regime in PeopleHub during the declaration window in April.
4.2 Investment declarations are submitted in April, and proofs are submitted between 1 January and 15 February. Income tax is recalculated in February and March.
4.3 Form 16 is issued by 15 June after the end of the financial year.

## 5. Loss of pay
For each day of unpaid absence, the salary is reduced by the monthly gross fixed pay divided by 30. Unpaid absence includes Leave Without Pay and unauthorised absence.

## 6. Salary advance
6.1 An employee who has completed 1 year of service may request a salary advance of up to one month's net salary, for a medical emergency, a family emergency or a housing deposit.
6.2 The advance is approved by the HR head and recovered in 3 equal monthly instalments, starting from the next payroll. The company does not charge interest.
6.3 Only one advance can be outstanding at any time.

## 7. Increments and variable pay
Increments take effect on 1 July. Variable pay is paid with the June payroll. The amounts are decided under the Performance Review Policy. An employee who is on notice period on the payout date is not eligible for variable pay.

## 8. On-call and shift allowances
8.1 Employees in production support teams who are on call receive Rs 1,500 for each week of on-call duty, on top of salary.
8.2 Employees who work in the night shift (between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM) in customer support teams receive a night shift allowance of Rs 8,000 per month.
8.3 Allowances are paid in the following month's payroll, after the manager confirms the duty roster.

## 9. Reimbursements through payroll
Approved claims under the Expense and Travel Reimbursement Policy are paid with the salary on the last working day if they are approved by the 20th of the month. Reimbursements are paid without tax up to the limits allowed by the Income Tax Act.

## 10. Errors in pay
If you find an error in your payslip, write to payroll@acme-analytics.example within 30 days. If the company has overpaid you, the recovery will be made in instalments agreed with you, and not more than 25% of the monthly net salary per month, unless you are leaving.

## 11. Confidentiality of pay
Employees should keep their compensation confidential and not discuss it with colleagues in a way that harms the workplace. This does not prevent anyone from discussing pay concerns with HR or exercising legal rights.
