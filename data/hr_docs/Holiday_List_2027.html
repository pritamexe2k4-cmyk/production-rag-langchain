2. Holidays at the Bengaluru and Pune offices

The Telangana Formation Day and Bathukamma holidays apply only to the Hyderabad office. In their place:

  • the Bengaluru office observes Kannada Rajyotsava on Monday, 1 November 2027, and Maha Navami on Thursday, 14 October 2027;
  • the Pune office observes Shivaji Jayanti on Friday, 19 February 2027, and Anant Chaturdashi on Wednesday, 15 September 2027.

All other holidays in the table are common to all three offices, so every office has 11 holidays.

3. Holidays that fall on a weekend

Five public holidays in 2027 fall on a Saturday or a Sunday and are therefore not in the table above: May Day (Saturday, 1 May), Independence Day (Sunday, 15 August), Ganesh Chaturthi (Sunday, 5 September), Gandhi Jayanti (Saturday, 2 October) and Christmas (Saturday, 25 December). No compensatory holiday is given for a holiday that falls on a weekend.

5. Working on a holiday

An employee who is required to work on a company holiday, for example for a client release, earns a Compensatory Off, as explained in the Leave Policy. The manager must ask in writing.

6. Year-end

There is no company-wide shutdown at the end of the year. Employees who want to take time off between 27 and 31 December 2027 should apply for Privilege Leave in the usual way, and teams should plan coverage in advance.
