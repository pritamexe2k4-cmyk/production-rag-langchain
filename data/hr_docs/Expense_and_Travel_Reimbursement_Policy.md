# Expense and Travel Reimbursement Policy
Document type: policy
Category: expenses
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Version: 3.2
Owner: Finance, Acme Analytics Pvt Ltd
Note: Fictional sample document created for testing a RAG system. Acme Analytics Pvt Ltd is not a real company.

## 1. Purpose and principles
1.1 This policy explains what business expenses Acme Analytics Pvt Ltd reimburses, the limits, and how to claim.
1.2 Employees are expected to spend company money as carefully as their own. Expenses must be reasonable, necessary for business and supported by receipts.
1.3 Employee bands (A to E) are defined in the Employee Handbook. Most limits in this policy depend on the band.

## 2. How to claim
2.1 All claims are submitted in ExpenseDesk, with the bill or receipt attached.
2.2 A claim must be submitted within 30 days of the date of the expense. Claims submitted 31 to 60 days after the expense need approval from the Finance Controller. Claims older than 60 days are rejected.
2.3 A receipt is required for every expense of Rs 500 or more. If a receipt is lost, the employee can submit a signed self-declaration for amounts up to Rs 2,000, but only once per quarter.
2.4 Approved claims are paid with the monthly payroll. Claims that are fully approved by the 20th of a month are paid on the last working day of that month. Claims approved after the 20th are paid in the following month.

## 3. Approval limits
| Total claim amount | Who approves |
|---|---|
| Up to Rs 10,000 | Reporting manager |
| Rs 10,001 to Rs 50,000 | Reporting manager and department head |
| Above Rs 50,000 | Reporting manager, department head and Finance Controller |

An employee cannot approve their own claim. If the approver is on leave for more than 3 days, the claim goes to the skip-level manager.

## 4. Domestic travel
4.1 All business travel must be approved by the manager before booking, and booked through TravelDesk, the company's travel partner, at least 7 days in advance. Last-minute bookings need a short justification.
4.2 Air travel is in economy class for Bands A, B and C. Band D and Band E employees may travel in premium economy on flights longer than 4 hours. Train travel is in AC 2-tier, or Executive Chair Car for day trains.
4.3 Hotel stays must be booked through TravelDesk. The maximum room rate per night, including taxes, is as follows.

| Band | Metro cities (per night) | Other cities (per night) | Meal allowance (per day of travel) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Band A | Rs 4,500 | Rs 3,000 | Rs 800 |
| Band B | Rs 6,000 | Rs 4,000 | Rs 1,000 |
| Band C | Rs 8,000 | Rs 5,500 | Rs 1,300 |
| Band D | Rs 11,000 | Rs 7,500 | Rs 1,800 |
| Band E | Rs 15,000 | Rs 10,000 | Rs 2,200 |

4.4 Metro cities are Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata and Pune. All other cities are treated as other cities.
4.5 The meal allowance is a fixed amount for each day on which the employee is travelling overnight. It does not need bills. If the company or the client provides a meal, the allowance is reduced by half for that day.

## 5. International travel
5.1 International travel needs the approval of the Vice President and Finance, and a valid business visa.
5.2 Hotel limits per night and daily meal allowance for international travel are in US dollars:

| Band | Hotel (USD per night) | Meal allowance (USD per day) |
|---|---|---|
| Band A and Band B | 150 | 50 |
| Band C | 200 | 60 |
| Band D | 260 | 75 |
| Band E | 350 | 100 |

5.3 Employees must buy travel insurance through TravelDesk for every international trip. Foreign exchange is available as a travel card; cash advances in foreign currency are not given.

## 6. Local conveyance
6.1 Cab, auto and metro fares for business trips are reimbursed on actual bills. Commuting between home and the office is not reimbursable.
6.2 If an employee uses their own vehicle for official work, the rate is Rs 9 per km for a car and Rs 4 per km for a two-wheeler. The employee must submit the trip details with the starting and ending locations.
6.3 Parking and toll charges on official trips are reimbursed on receipts.

## 7. Telephone, internet and home office
| Allowance | Amount |
|---|---|
| Mobile phone bill, monthly | Band A Rs 750; Band B Rs 1,000; Band C Rs 1,500; Band D and E Rs 2,000 |
| Broadband (hybrid and remote roles), monthly | Rs 1,000 against the bill |
| Home office setup, one time | Rs 10,000, once every 3 years |

## 8. Client entertainment and team events
8.1 Client entertainment needs prior approval from the department head. The limit is Rs 2,500 per person per occasion.
8.2 Team lunches and team-building activities are limited to Rs 1,200 per person per event, and each team may hold two such events per quarter.

## 9. Travel advance
9.1 An employee can take a travel advance of up to 75% of the estimated travel cost, after the trip is approved.
9.2 The advance must be settled in ExpenseDesk within 10 working days of returning from the trip. An unsettled advance is recovered from the next salary.

## 10. Expenses that are not reimbursed
The following are never reimbursed: alcohol, fines and penalties (including traffic fines), personal items, minibar and room entertainment, spa and laundry charges for trips shorter than 5 nights, expenses of family members who accompany the employee, and any expense without a valid business purpose.

## 11. Misuse
A false or inflated claim is gross misconduct and may lead to disciplinary action, including termination of employment, under the Code of Conduct. The company may recover the amount from the salary or the full and final settlement.
