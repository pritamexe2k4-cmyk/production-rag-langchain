1. Welcome

1.1 Welcome to Acme Analytics Pvt Ltd. This guide helps you through your first 90 days. Your Human Resources Business Partner (HRBP), your manager and your onboarding buddy will help you at each step.

1.2 You will receive your welcome email with login details three working days before your joining date.

2. Before you join: documents to submit

Please submit scanned copies through the onboarding portal at least 2 working days before your joining date, and bring the originals on day 1 for verification.

  • Government photo ID (Aadhaar card) and PAN card;
  • Address proof;
  • Educational certificates and mark sheets;
  • Relieving letter or resignation acceptance from your last employer, and the last 3 payslips;
  • 4 passport-size photographs;
  • A cancelled cheque or bank passbook page for salary credit;
  • Form 11 for Provident Fund, and your UAN if you have worked before.

4. Background verification

4.1 Your offer is subject to a satisfactory background verification (BGV), done by an external agency. It covers identity, education and employment for the last 7 years, and criminal records.

4.2 BGV normally takes 7 to 10 working days. Please give the details accurately. A mismatch between the information you provided and the verification result can lead to withdrawal of the offer or termination of employment.

5. Probation and the first 90 days

5.1 Every new employee goes through a 6-month probation period. At the end of the sixth month, your manager reviews your performance and, if it is satisfactory, you are confirmed in writing.

5.2 A 30-60-90 day plan is set up with your manager in the first week.

  • In the first 30 days: learn the product, the team and the tools; finish mandatory training.
  • By day 60: take ownership of a small piece of work.
  • By day 90: deliver a project with limited supervision, and review it with your manager.

5.3 During probation, Privilege Leave can be taken only for up to 3 consecutive days, as stated in the Leave Policy. Notice period during probation is 30 days.

7. Work schedule and hybrid rules

In the first 60 days after joining you are expected to be in the office 4 days a week. After that, the standard hybrid schedule applies: 3 days in the office, with Tuesday and Wednesday as anchor days. See the Hybrid Work and Work From Home Policy.

8. Salary, benefits and insurance

8.1 Salary is credited on the last working day of each month. If you join after the 20th of a month, your first salary will be calculated for the days you worked and paid with the next month's payroll.

8.2 You are covered under the Group Medical Insurance from day 1. You must add your dependants in the insurance portal within 15 days of joining. After that, they can be added only during the annual enrolment window in April, or within 30 days of a life event such as marriage or the birth of a child.

8.3 You will receive an email about your Provident Fund account in your first month.

9. Your equipment and accounts

Band A and Band B employees receive a standard 14-inch laptop. Employees in Band C and above can choose between a standard and a performance laptop. Your laptop is encrypted and managed by IT. You will get an email account, a chat account, VPN access and multi-factor authentication on day 1. Raise any IT issue with the IT helpdesk at helpdesk@acme-analytics.example or extension 4000.
